Does the voice sound too robotic?

The site automatically selects the best Romanian voice available. You can also choose another installed Romanian voice from the Romanian voice list above. For a more natural result, install an Enhanced, Premium or Natural Romanian voice, then reload the page.

  • iPhone/Mac: System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices.
  • Android: Settings → Text-to-speech → Install voice data → Romanian.
  • Windows: Settings → Time & language → Speech → Add voices → Romanian.

Audio uses a Romanian voice installed on your browser or device.
